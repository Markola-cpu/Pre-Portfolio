05 / Journal

My Reflections &
Learning Experiences

R.

Overall Reflection

Throughout these activities, I explored different ways to express ideas through art and digital design. From pencil sketches, color studies, and character work to vector graphics, logos, and posters, I practiced observing details, arranging visual elements, and refining my work. I learned that creativity takes patience and experimentation. As a BTLED-ICT student, I can use these skills to create clear, engaging visual materials and support learners in expressing their own ideas. This portfolio reflects my progress and encourages me to keep learning.

01

What creative skills improved throughout these activities?

I practiced sketching and tracing, using color, arranging shapes, and combining typography with imagery. I am becoming more thoughtful about composition and how design choices communicate an idea.

02

What challenges helped me learn the most?

Learning digital tools, keeping lines and shapes clear, and balancing color and layout challenged me. Trying, reviewing, and refining my work helped me become more patient with the creative process.

03

How can these experiences support my future teaching career?

I can use these skills to make visual aids, posters, and digital learning materials. I can also guide students as they explore design tools, communicate ideas visually, and learn through revision.
